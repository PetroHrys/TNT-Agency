The Agency provides the following recruitment, staffing and workforce services in accordance with Czech and applicable European Union law. Services that require a permit under Act No. 435/2004 Coll. are provided only within the scope of the permission actually held:

  • Recruitment and direct placement — mediation of employment between employers and job seekers for permanent or fixed-term employment contracts.

  • Professional, specialist and technical recruitment — recruitment for qualified technical and specialist roles and for first-line operational management, including checking the qualifications and authorisations a given role requires.
  • Workforce solutions — support with defining the role, setting up the recruitment and selection process, induction, and the employment of foreign nationals and the related permits.
